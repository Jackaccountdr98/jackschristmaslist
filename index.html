BenQ ZOWIE XL2566X+

The 400 Hz esports monitor

A 24-inch, 1080p monitor built for one job: making fast shooters look as clear and smooth as possible. Its Fast TN panel runs at 400 Hz, and ZOWIE's DyAc 2 backlight strobing cuts motion blur so moving targets stay sharp. It comes with a height-adjustable PROGamer stand, a removable shielding hood and the S Switch controller for swapping settings quickly.

What reviewers say

4.7 / 5 from 764 shopper ratings on PcComponentes (95% would recommend)

Dot Esports

Calls it one of the best esports monitors. Colours are clearly better than ZOWIE's older 144 Hz model, though it won't match an OLED, and the reviewer happily uses it for everyday work too.

3DTested

Praises the DyAc 2 strobe and the premium stand with position markings. It costs well under a comparable OLED, but the gamma options are a letdown and TN is showing its age.

Business Today

Finds the new TN panel more colourful than earlier ZOWIE models, and football stayed sharp in fast pans. The verdict: superb for competitive play, not a general-purpose screen.

PcComponentes shoppers

Owners love the smoothness, DyAc and the S Switch profiles. The common trade-offs are weaker TN colours and a dimmer picture when strobing is on.

SteelSeries Apex Pro TKL Gen 3

The keyboard with adjustable keys

A wired, tenkeyless gaming keyboard with OmniPoint 3.0 magnetic switches, so you can choose how far each key must travel before it registers. Rapid Trigger makes keys reset instantly, a small OLED screen shows settings at a glance, and a magnetic wrist rest comes in the box. Everything is set up in the free SteelSeries GG software, with RGB lighting per key.

What reviewers say

TechRadar

Build quality and gaming performance are the highlights and it's a joy to use. It's expensive at £209, and they found it less appealing for typing than for gaming.

PC Gamer

Pricey for the size, with no wireless mode or USB passthrough. Even so, the aluminium top, switches, OLED and software add up to a true enthusiast keyboard.

ProSettings

Still one of the best mainstream gaming boards; the tester couldn't notice any latency difference against today's top models. The case is plastic, and setup happens in GG.

CGMagazine

Rates the typing feel among the best they've used, backed by Rapid Trigger and a smooth, weighted feel. Their verdict is that it's a no-brainer for a feature-packed keyboard.
